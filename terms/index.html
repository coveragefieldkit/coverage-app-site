2. What the App does

COVERAGE is a professional reference and pre-production tool for cinematographers, camera assistants, and directors. Its features include, but are not limited to:

  • Visualising lens image circle coverage across a wide catalogue of cinema and photographic sensors, in spherical and anamorphic modes.
  • Computing depth-of-field, hyperfocal distance, field of view, and related optical parameters.
  • Providing a "director's viewfinder" that overlays framelines, aspect ratios, and sensor crops onto the device camera preview.
  • Capturing reference frames ("shots"), annotating them with lens, focal length, focus distance, aspect ratio, GPS coordinates, and compass heading, and organising them into projects and shot lists.
  • Exporting shot lists and coverage sheets to PDF for use on set.
  • Suggesting rental options and recommending accessories such as matte boxes, clip-on filters, and support rigs, where compatibility rules are defined in the catalogue.

The App is a planning and reference tool. It is not a light meter, an exposure meter, a certification of optical performance, or a substitute for professional judgement, camera tests, and on-set verification.

3. Reference data — provided "as is"

The App ships with a catalogue of lens and camera reference data ("Reference Data"), including:

  • Sensor dimensions, pixel counts, and recording modes.
  • Lens specifications, including image-circle diameter, focal length, close focus distance, T-stop, filter thread, and physical dimensions.
  • Coverage geometry, framelines, and compatibility notes.
  • Third-party rental-house or accessory information where surfaced in-app.

Reference Data is compiled from publicly available manufacturer publications, service documents, and reputable industry sources. Manufacturers change specifications, publish inconsistent figures, and occasionally publish figures that differ from measured behaviour. Coverage geometry in particular is an approximation of a complex optical system.

Reference Data is provided on an "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" basis, without any warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose. You are solely responsible for verifying any Reference Data that informs a production decision — including, but not limited to, camera and lens tests, back-focus checks, coverage checks on the actual sensor and recording mode you intend to use, and physical fit-up of accessories. Do not rely on the App as the sole basis for a rental order, a camera build, a shot design, or an on-set decision.
