Common questions

A lens spec or a rental house listing is wrong

Email the lens, the camera and what's wrong; a link to the maker's or the rental house's own page helps. Corrections go into the next catalogue update.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile and tap DELETE ACCOUNT. This removes your sign-in and everything synced to it. Shots and LUTs on your device stay until you delete the app.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your device without one. An account only syncs your theme, favourites and projects between devices.

Where are my shots stored?

On your device only. They leave it when you share or export them yourself.
