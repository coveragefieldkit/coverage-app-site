About

The camera department's field kit.

Coverage Field Kit is an iPhone app for the camera department. Pick a camera, a sensor mode and a lens, and it draws the image circle that lens throws onto that sensor, to scale, with light falloff and a warning when the corners vignette. The catalog holds 2,075 cine lenses, 71 camera bodies and 749 sensor modes.

On set, the director's viewfinder zooms the phone's cameras to the cine lens's field of view and draws framelines and camera settings over the live image. Stills and clips are saved with the lens, framelines, focus, camera settings, tilt, compass heading and GPS. Every lens page lists the rental houses that carry it: 474 in 73 countries.

Coverage Field Kit is made by Daniel Ocanto Hernandez, an independent developer. It is out on the App Store on 3 November 2026.

Features

What it does.

Coverage

Any lens on any sensor

The image circle drawn to scale over the exact sensor mode, with light falloff at the T-stop you set and a warning when the corners vignette.

Compare A | B

Two setups side by side

Two lenses on one camera, or one lens on two cameras, with the specs in two columns, row for row.

Viewfinder

Frame through the cine lens

The phone's cameras zoom to the cine lens's field of view, with framelines, a camera-settings HUD and looks (LUTs) over the live image.

Shot log

Every frame keeps its setup

Stills and clips save with the lens, framelines, focus, camera settings, tilt, compass heading and GPS. Export them as a shot-list PDF.

Projects

One project per shoot

Camera, sensor mode, lens list and crew credits together, shared as a single file.

Reports

Print-ready paperwork

A4 reports for a lens, a set or a project, and landscape A|B comparison sheets.

Rent this lens

Who carries it

474 rental houses in 73 countries, listed lens by lens, with their websites and contact details.

MTF / VFX

Data for the VFX team

MTF and lens data exported as a Nuke .nk file and CSV.
