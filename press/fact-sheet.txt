ABOUT
-----
Coverage is an iPhone app for the camera department. Pick a
camera, a sensor mode and a lens, and it draws the image circle that lens
throws onto that sensor, to scale, with light falloff and a warning when
the corners vignette. The catalog holds 2,075 cine lenses, 71 camera
bodies and 749 sensor modes.

On set, the director's viewfinder zooms the phone's cameras to the cine
lens's field of view and draws framelines and camera settings over the
live image. Stills and clips are saved with the lens, framelines, focus,
camera settings, tilt, compass heading and GPS. Every lens page lists the
rental houses that carry it: 474 in 73 countries.

Coverage is made by Daniel Ocanto Hernandez, an independent
developer. It is out on the App Store on 3 November 2026.


FEATURES
--------
- Coverage: any cine lens on any sensor mode, image circle drawn to scale,
  light falloff at the T-stop you set, vignette warnings.
- Compare A|B: two lenses or two cameras side by side, specs row for row.
- Director's viewfinder: the phone's cameras zoom to the cine lens's field
  of view, with framelines, a camera-settings HUD and looks (LUTs).
- Stills and clips saved with lens, framelines, focus, camera settings,
  tilt, compass heading and GPS.
- Shot log and shot-list PDF.
- Projects with crew credits and lens lists.
- Print-ready A4 lens reports and landscape A|B comparison sheets.
- Rent this lens: 474 rental houses in 73 countries, lens by lens.
- MTF/VFX export: Nuke .nk and CSV.
