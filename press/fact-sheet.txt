ABOUT
-----
Coverage is an iPhone app for the camera department. Pick a
camera, a sensor mode and a lens, and it draws the image circle that lens
throws onto that sensor, to scale, with light falloff and a warning when
the corners vignette. It also checks whether a matte box will show in
the frame, and what an expander behind the lens does to the focal length,
T-stop and coverage. The catalog holds 2,424 cine lenses, 73 camera
bodies and 759 sensor modes.

On set, the director's viewfinder zooms the phone's cameras to the cine
lens's field of view and draws framelines, camera settings and the
day's sun path over the live image, with EI, ND and white balance
previewed as you change them. Stills and clips are saved with the lens, framelines,
camera settings, tilt, compass heading and GPS. Every lens page lists the
rental houses that carry it: 474 in 73 countries.

Coverage is made by Daniel Ocanto Hernandez, an independent
developer. It is out on the App Store on 3 November 2026.


FEATURES
--------
- Coverage: any cine lens on any sensor mode, image circle drawn to scale,
  light falloff at the T-stop you set, vignette warnings.
- Compare A|B: two lenses or two cameras side by side, specs row for row.
- Matte box check: whether a matte box vignettes wide open or the lens
  front won't fit. Ten boxes from ARRI, Bright Tangerine, Lightwidow and
  Tilta. A model from the box's geometry; test borderline setups.
- Expanders and extenders: 29 rear converters; the new focal length,
  T-stop and image circle on your sensor. A model from the converter's
  optics; test borderline setups.
- Director's viewfinder: the phone's cameras zoom to the cine lens's field
  of view, with framelines, a camera-settings HUD and looks (.cube,
  ARRI .aml, ASC CDL).
- Exposure, live: EI, ND and white balance previewed in the viewfinder as
  you change them. A preview, not a light meter.
- Sun path: the day's sun path over the frame for any date and time,
  saved with every shot with sunrise, sunset and golden hour.
- Stills and clips saved with lens, framelines, camera settings, tilt,
  compass heading and GPS.
- Shot log and shot-list PDF.
- Projects with crew credits and lens lists.
- Print-ready A4 lens reports and landscape A|B comparison sheets.
- Rent this lens: 474 rental houses in 73 countries, lens by lens.
- MTF/VFX export: Nuke .nk and CSV.
